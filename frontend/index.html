Protocol
#StimulusResponseRT×medRel.

Carmine marks a disturbed response: a non-timing indicator, or reaction time at least twice the median, or a long reaction time together with another indicator. Relatedness is recorded for later analysis but does not flag on its own. A grey LONG RT tag alone means above the subject's median (Jung's probable mean), which by itself is weak. The ×med column is how many times the median that reaction took. A shaded row is the word before a flagged one and is queued for investigation. A dot by the number marks a stimulus tagged as emotionally loaded. Number is presentation order; the export also carries each word's position in its source list. Grey tags give the association type in Jung & Riklin's scheme: inner (meaning), outer (habit or co-occurrence), clang (sound), remainder (failure, repetition, far-fetched), egocentric (personal reference). RT is shown in seconds; the export also carries milliseconds and Jung's fifths of a second.
